# Project Brief: RTP Reading Club (fictional)

> Everything on this site is made up for a workshop at All Things Open 2026.
> The club, the reading room and the gathering are fictional. The books are real
> novels by Matt Dinniman.

## Goal
A single web page for a pretend book club. It shows what the club is, when it
meets next, what to read before then, and which book comes next.

## Page content
**Heading:** RTP Reading Club

**Sections, in this order:**
1. **About**: a short, friendly paragraph about the (fictional) club, with a
   note that the club is not real.
2. **Next Gathering**
   - Date and time: October 24, 2026, 10:30 AM
   - Place: RTP Community Reading Room (fictional; no address)
   - Reading: Chapters 10-15 of *This Inevitable Ruin* by Matt Dinniman
     (Dungeon Crawler Carl, Book 7)
3. **Next Book Read**: *A Parade of Horribles* by Matt Dinniman
   (Dungeon Crawler Carl, Book 8)

**Main button:** a button near the top labeled something like "See the next
gathering" that scrolls down to the Next Gathering section.

## Rules and limits
- One `index.html` file with its styles included. Plain HTML/CSS and, only if
  needed, a little JavaScript.
- No sign-up, payment, database, email, accounts, forms or paid services.
- No real addresses, phone numbers, emails or people's names (apart from the author).
- Works when the file is opened straight from the computer, with no server.
- Easy to read on a phone and on a laptop.

## Completion checks (what you should see in your browser)
1. Opening `index.html` shows the heading **"RTP Reading Club"** at the top.
2. Three sections appear in order: **About**, **Next Gathering**, **Next Book Read**.
3. **Next Gathering** shows October 24, 2026 at 10:30 AM, the RTP Community
   Reading Room, and Chapters 10-15 of *This Inevitable Ruin* (Dungeon Crawler
   Carl Book 7).
4. Clicking the main button moves the page down so the **Next Gathering**
   section is in view.
5. The page clearly says it is fictional and has no sign-up form, payment,
   email link or login anywhere.

## Out of scope
More pages, a calendar, RSVPs, member lists, comments and anything that saves data.
